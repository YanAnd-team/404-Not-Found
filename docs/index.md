# Tomb of Mask

A tribute to the classic mobile game *Tomb of the Mask*.

Dive into ancient tombs and slide through corridors — dodge enemies and traps, collect stars, and reach the exit across 5 challenging levels. No jumping: just directional sliding until you hit a wall.

Built with **C++** and **raylib** as part of the Game Design and Development Bachelor's Degree at Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC).

## Features

- 5 hand-crafted levels with increasing difficulty
- Directional sliding mechanics — no jumping
- 3-star rating system per level
- **Enemies:** Ghosts patrolling corridors
- **Traps:** Fixed spikes, trigger spikes, gun traps firing projectiles
- **Debug mode:** Toggle hitboxes with `B`, load any level with `1`–`5`
